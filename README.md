## About

Due Two is a technology startup founded by two Computer Science students with a shared interest in building practical technology solutions.

Our work brings together software development, intelligent systems, and hardware integration to explore ideas, develop prototypes, and create useful digital experiences.

## What We Work On

- **Artificial Intelligence & Computer Vision**
- **Software Engineering & Web Development**
- **Robotics & Embedded Systems**
- **Intelligent and Interactive Prototypes**
